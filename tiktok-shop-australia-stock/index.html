Who this is for

Hosts. You go live, you need units in the room, and you buy them rather than waiting on affiliate samples. TikTok live sellers and anyone else who already moves stock in a show.

Creators who only want commission do not need this page. Get on the list as an affiliate and say no to stock. You’ll hear when TikTok Shop Australia moves — not a wholesale pitch.

What “stock” means here

A pack is a lot with photos and counts. You look, then take it or leave it. Same habit as a proper wholesale desk. Not a PDF. Not a course. Not a dropshipping diagram.

Categories people ask about: clothing, beauty, gadgets, home, mystery / blind boxes. Tell us yours on the form, including one we did not list. We are not taking orders for a line that does not exist yet, and we are not publishing pack sizes or prices until there is a lot to show.

What this page is not

  • Not an open TikTok Shop Australia catalogue. Checkout in the Australian app is unconfirmed. See how to sell on TikTok Shop Australia.
  • Not a fee card. TikTok has not published Australian seller fees. We will not invent one so a stock story sounds finished.
  • Not the Rage Wholesale clothing grid. Branded, vintage and fashion lots for resellers live on Rage Wholesale. Different site. Different form. Do not expect those lines repeated here.
