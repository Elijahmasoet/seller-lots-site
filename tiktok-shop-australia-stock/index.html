Quick Answers

Can I buy TikTok Shop Australia stock here today?

No. There’s no open catalogue and TikTok Shop Australia isn’t live yet. Get on the list and say you want packs. When a lot is real, that’s who we call.

Can brands list product for creators?

Yes — that’s the other side of the list. Australian brands get matched with creators / affiliates who distribute. No invented fees or pack prices until there’s a real lot.
