Quick Answers

How do I sell my products / stock on TikTok Shop Australia?

TikTok Shop is not live for Australian domestic selling yet. Australian businesses can’t open a local AU→AU TikTok Shop storefront, and shoppers can’t check out through a local in-app Shop. TikTok has not published a confirmed Australian launch date.

While you wait, join the Seller Lots list as an Australian Brand so you’re tagged for creator and affiliate match when the market moves. Brands are here for match. You do not need to buy wholesale packs to get ready. Only pick Creator Buying Stock if you want hosts buying inventory packs. That lane is separate and optional.

Seller Lots (sellerlots.com.au) is an independent Australian prep list and stock desk from the team behind Rage Wholesale, not TikTok and not Seller Center. Get ready on the waitlist and choose the Brand chip.

Is TikTok Shop Australia live?

No. As of this page’s last update, Australians can’t buy through a local TikTok Shop checkout and Australian businesses can’t open a domestic AU→AU TikTok Shop storefront. TikTok has not published a confirmed Australian launch date.

Is there a confirmed launch date? Is Q1 locked in?

No confirmed date from TikTok. Soft Q1 talk you may hear is industry chatter only, not a TikTok announcement. We’ll update this page when TikTok confirms something real.

Do I need stock to get ready for TikTok Shop Australia?

No. Only people who pick Creator Buying Stock should expect pack talk. Commission-only creators and affiliates can get ready without cartons. Brands join for creator match, not because they must buy wholesale lots. Seller Lots keeps stock outreach limited to Creator Buying Stock.

What’s the difference between brand, affiliate/creator, Creator Buying Stock, and curious?

Brand = you’re here for creator/affiliate match, not automatically as a wholesale buyer. Affiliate/creator = commission path, no carton required. Creator Buying Stock = you want inventory packs when a real lot exists. Curious = short updates only, no lots pitch.

Is Seller Lots part of TikTok? Who runs it?

No. Seller Lots is an independent Australian list and stock desk from the team behind Rage Wholesale. Joining does not create a TikTok Shop seller account and is not an official TikTok programme.

Does joining Seller Lots open a TikTok Shop for me?

No. You’re tagged for when TikTok Shop Australia status changes, for brand and creator match if that’s your lane, and for packs only if you chose Creator Buying Stock.

What happens after I get on the Seller Lots list?

You’re tagged by the role you chose. Creator Buying Stock hears when a real lot is available. Brands and affiliates hear about matches. Everyone else stays in the loop for TikTok Shop Australia status changes. Curious signups stay short and don’t get a wholesale pitch.
