What TikTok Shop is

TikTok Shop is checkout inside TikTok. In a country where it is live, a viewer can buy from a video, a live, or a shop tab without leaving the app. Sellers list products. Creators can earn commission for promoting them. Hosts sell on a live.

That is not the same as running TikTok ads. It is not a link in your bio to your own website. Ads and a link-out still work in Australia. In-app TikTok Shop checkout is a different product, and TikTok runs it country by country. A shop in one country is not a shop in Australia.

Is TikTok Shop open in Australia?

Not as a local shop. As of 26 October 2026:

  • TikTok has not published a confirmed Australian launch date. Industry chatter points to a likely Q1 window — that’s not a TikTok announcement. Watch this space. This page changes when they do.
  • A market tracker updated 9 October 2026 reported that seller.tiktok.com did not list Australia as a live local market. That write-up is theirs, not TikTok’s. Lists move. Check the seller centre yourself before you plan a storefront.
  • The seller centre you land on may belong to a country where TikTok Shop is already live. A US, UK or Singapore registration screen is not an Australian application.
  • There is no Australian fee card from TikTok.

People also mix this up with other sites that put “TikTok Shop” in a domain. tiktokshop.com.au presents itself as TKSHOP, a Melbourne marketplace. That is not TikTok Shop in the app, and it is not Seller Lots. Read who you are signing with.

If Australian shoppers can complete a real in-app TikTok Shop checkout, this page is behind. Tell us on the list and we will correct it.

What you can do now

You can get ready. You cannot “go live on TikTok Shop Australia” this week. The local checkout is not there.

  1. Pick what you are. Host who buys stock. Affiliate or creator who wants commission. Brand or supplier. Or just watching. The form asks so we do not pitch lots to people who do not want them.
  2. Keep the audience, if TikTok is where you post. TikTok Shop, when it exists, sits on content. A dead account does not become a shop. This is not a content school. Post if you already mean to post.
  3. Name the category. Clothing, beauty, gadgets, home, mystery, or something else. Say it on the form.
  4. Stay legal on sales you already make. If you sell goods to people in Australia today, on any channel, the Australian Consumer Law still applies to those sales. ABN and GST questions belong with the ATO and your accountant. That is separate from TikTok Shop rules, which are not published for Australia.
  5. Touch stock only if you buy it. Creators do not need a garage full of units to join. Hosts who want packs should say so. Read TikTok Shop Australia stock.
  6. Get on the list. Name, mobile, city, tag. That is the useful step while the date is unconfirmed. Get on the TikTok Shop Australia list.

Hosts, creators and brands are not the same lead

Hosts. You buy inventory and sell it on a live. You care about packs, photos and counts. On the form, choose host, and say yes if you want stock.

Affiliates and creators. You promote for commission. You may never want a carton. Choose affiliate. If you do not want stock, say no. You stay on updates.

Brands and suppliers. You have product and you want a channel. Choose brand. We read these. There is no open Australian seller application for us to forward you to.

Curious. You want the news. Choose curious. You will not get a wholesale pitch.

Fees and dates we will not invent

Other countries publish their own seller rules. People copy those numbers into Australian advice. Do not. We will not put any of the following on this page:

  • A seller fee, commission cut, or payment percentage for Australia.
  • A confirmed launch day. We’re preparing for a likely Q1 window from industry chatter; TikTok has not published a date. Rumours are not a launch.
  • A claim that a US, UK or Southeast Asian seller rule applies here.
  • A claim that you can register an Australian TikTok Shop account today.
  • A claim that you qualify for cross-border selling. Where TikTok offers it, eligibility depends on your business. It is not a local Australian shop, and it does not mean Australian shoppers can check out in the app.

If a seller page quotes a commission, read which country that page is for. A number on another country’s page is that country’s number.
