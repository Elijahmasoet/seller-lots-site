Quick Answers

Do I need stock to get on the list?

No. Creators buying stock say yes to packs. Creators on commission only say no. Brands are here for creator match, not wholesale cartons. We don’t pitch lots to people who said no.

Is Seller Lots part of TikTok?

No. Independent Australian list and stock desk. From the team behind Rage Wholesale.

How do brands and creators get matched?

Same list, two sides. Brands get matched with creators for affiliate. Creators can buy stock for lives or promote for commission. You’re tagged by what you picked.

What happens after I get on the list?

You’re tagged by role. Creators who asked for stock hear when there’s a lot. Brands and affiliates hear about matches. Everyone else hears when TikTok Shop Australia moves.
