The desk

About Seller Lots

Seller Lots is the list and stock desk for TikTok Shop Australia. From the team behind Rage Wholesale.

Not the clothing shop

Rage Wholesale supplies clothing lots to Australian resellers. That business has its own site and its own enquiry form. Seller Lots does not list that catalogue. Clothing leads do not land on this list. This list does not land on the clothing sheet.

Backed by Rage Wholesale means the same people, a different offer. It does not mean “a Rage brand” and it does not mean the vintage or branded grid moved here.

What we do now

We take a tagged list of people in Australia who care about TikTok Shop.

  • Hosts who buy stock for lives.
  • Affiliates and creators who want commission later.
  • Brands and suppliers.
  • People who are only watching the launch.

When TikTok confirms something, the list can hear it. When there is a lot for a host who asked, that host hears about the lot. Curious people are not pitched stock. Affiliates who said no are not pitched stock.

What we will not do

We will not pretend the Australian shop is open. We will not invent a launch date. We will not invent seller fees. We will not sell a course about how to sell. The plain version of the timing sits on how to sell on TikTok Shop Australia. The stock version sits on TikTok Shop Australia stock.

The name

The name is Seller Lots. Australia is the market: in the copy, and on sellerlots.com.au once that domain is bought. The mark says SL. It does not say TikTok, and we are not TikTok.

If you want clothing wholesale today, use Rage Wholesale. If you want the TikTok Shop Australia list, get on the list.
