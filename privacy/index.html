Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Plain-language summary for Australia. Not legal advice.

Who we are

Seller Lots is an Australian list and stock desk for people interested in TikTok Shop Australia. From the team behind Rage Wholesale. This list is separate from Rage Wholesale’s clothing enquiries.

Contact: hello@sellerlots.com.au. That mailbox works once sellerlots.com.au is live.

What we collect

If you get on the list, the form collects:

  • Full name, email and mobile number
  • City or postcode
  • Whether you are mainly a host, affiliate / creator, brand, or just curious
  • Where you sell or create today
  • Audience size, using follower bands
  • Categories you would sell, and any other category you type
  • Whether you want to buy stock packs
  • Anything you write in the notes
  • Ad and visit details when they are present: utm fields, gclid, the page you submitted from, the referrer, and the time of submission in Australia/Sydney

Why we collect it

So we know who you are before we contact you about TikTok Shop Australia. Hosts who say they want stock can be offered lots later. Everyone else can hear when TikTok Shop Australia moves. We use the tag so we do not pitch stock to people who did not ask.

We do not use this form to sell you Rage Wholesale clothing.

Who handles it

The form is processed by Formspree, which passes the submission to us. Formspree is a US service, so your details may be stored outside Australia before they reach us. We then keep them on our own sheet, in a tab used only for this list. We do not mix those rows with Rage Wholesale clothing leads.

This site does not run advertising or analytics tags at launch. If we add Google Ads or analytics later, we will update this page first.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t send stock offers to people who said they do not want stock.

How long we keep it

We keep list details while this list is running. Then we delete them or strip the identifying fields. You can ask us to delete sooner.

Access and deletion

Email hello@sellerlots.com.au to ask for a copy of your details, a correction, or deletion. If that mailbox is not receiving yet, submit the signup form again with the same email and write DELETE in the notes. We will treat that as the request.
